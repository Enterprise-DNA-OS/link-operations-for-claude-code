Buyer fit is agency fit, not a claim that an existing customer subscribes.

[Pitchbox pricing](https://pitchbox.com/pricing/) lists Scale at US$1,200 monthly billing and US$825 monthly equivalent billed annually. Twelve monthly payments are US$14,400; annual billing is US$9,900. A year commitment applies to either frequency. The selected audience is agencies on the five-figure monthly-billed Scale scenario, not every Pitchbox customer.

[Pitchbox export help](https://help.pitchbox.com/article/160-how-do-i-export-all-of-my-won-opportunities) documents an opportunity CSV containing domain, URL, milestone, tags and metrics. Exact labels are not promised. A synthetic fixture and explicit column map make the supported contract reviewable.

[Oxford Abstracts pricing](https://oxfordabstracts.com/pricing/) lists Professional at US$3,450 per event. A multi-event annual spend is possible, but was not treated as a verified invoice. [Rightsline FAQ](https://www.rightsline.com/resources/faq/) describes custom pricing based on modules and scope. Bynder was screened out as overlapping the existing brand asset family and offering no public price in the research.
